description: "I am an Assistant Professor in the Division of Food, Nutrition & Exercise Sciences (FNES) at the University of Missouri-Columbia, with appointments in the School of Medicine and the College of Agriculture, Food and Natural Resources. I received my PhD in Nutrition Science with a focus in clinical nutrition and ingestive behavior from Purdue University, an MS degree in Nutrition Science with a focus in community nutrition and dietetics from Syracuse University, and a BTech degree in Bioinformatics from JUIT in India. I completed postdoctoral training in microbiome and metabolomics research at the University of California, Merced. My training across nutrition science, omics, and bioinformatics allows me to design and lead a research program that integrates 1) human interventions testing whole foods, dietary patterns, and lifestyle effects, 2) mechanistic investigation through the gut microbiome and multi-omics approaches, and 3) examination of the contextual factors, including the food environment, food security, demographics, and behaviors, that shape variability in cardiometabolic responses to diet. I also study pre- and post-ingestive controls of food intake, and I lead the integration of multistate lifestyle and behavioral data as MU Head for USDA Multistate Project NC1193."
