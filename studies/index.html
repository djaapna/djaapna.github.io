Effects of Lean Beef on Cardiometabolic Health and Gut Microbiome

Participants are needed for a 6-week study examining the effects of lean beef on cardiometabolic health and gut microbiome in adults.

If you are interested and meet the criteria, please contact MuNepBeefStudy@missouri.edu. Study coordinator: Elaheh Rabbani.

Effects of Grapes on Immune and Gut Health in Obesity

Participants are needed for a 12-week study investigating the effects of grape consumption on immune and gut health in adults with obesity (only 8 weeks of active participation).

If you are interested and meet the criteria, please contact NepImmuneHealthStudy@missouri.edu. Study coordinator: Caitlyn Winn.
