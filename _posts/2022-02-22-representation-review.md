---
title: "Representation review in *Advances in Nutrition*"
date_label: "February 2022"
---

Dr. Dhillon, Sigry Ortiz Flores, and colleagues reviewed how racial and ethnic minority groups are represented in clinical nutrition interventions. The review was named an Editor's Choice in the ASN Publications Newsletter. Read the [paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/advances/nmac002).
